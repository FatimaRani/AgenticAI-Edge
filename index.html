Can it think smart and eat less energy?

Agentic AI @Edge is a physical AI demonstrator from TU Dresden. It sees a scene through a camera, reasons about it, and acts, all on small edge devices. Our GAMING and SAGE frameworks measure every watt, millisecond and gram of CO₂, and an agent switches to a leaner setup whenever it can.

The problem

AI is scaling fast. Its energy bill is scaling with it.

Large language models need a lot of computing power. Most tools that run them in production track speed and cost, but not energy or carbon. Operators see latency. Nobody sees the watts.

Europe is starting to ask for the numbers. Few tools can deliver them for AI at the edge.

  • Data centres must report. The recast EU Energy Efficiency Directive (2023) requires larger data centres to publish energy performance data.
  • AI providers must document. The EU AI Act asks providers of general-purpose AI models to document their models' energy consumption.
  • The gap is at the edge. More AI now runs on cameras, boards and laptops outside data centres. We measure energy, CO₂ and speed there, per answer and per frame.

Edge is not automatically greener

Energy is power multiplied by time. The small board sips power but answers slowly. The GPU draws more power but finishes sooner. Each box's area is the energy for one answer (SAGE study, 20 runs).

2026 research output

Four peer-reviewed papers, one chain of evidence.

Each paper answers one question, on the same hardware, with the same measurement method. Together they show where AI's energy goes and how an agent can take it back.

IEEE WCCCT 2026 · Qingdao

GAMING: A Green Computing Framework for Deploying LLM at the Edge

How do quantized LLMs compare on speed, energy and CO₂ on edge hardware?

17 runs, 9 models. Model choice alone changes energy per answer by up to 5.1×.

IEEE BDAI 2026 · Chongqing

Small footprint, big intelligence! SAGE: Agentic governance for energy-aware, high-performance LLMOps

Does a closed-loop, two-agent design pick sustainable configurations?

20 runs. The faster GPU used about a quarter less energy per request despite 7.5× the power.

GLOBECOM 2026 · Workshop WS-24

CARE: Carbon-Aware Reasoning at the Edge for Sustainable Connected Healthcare

Are compact edge LLMs fast and reliable enough for glucose-monitoring alerts?

Real data from 25 people with type 1 diabetes. Fastest setup answered in 3.3 s; a sensor reading arrives every 5 minutes.

IEEE CCNC 2027 · Las Vegas · 9–12 Jan

Think Green, Act Lean! An Agentic Sandbox for Autonomous Optimization of Edge LLMOps

Can an agent change a live AI deployment safely, without a human in the loop?

Every proposed change passes an evaluation gate first. If it fails, nothing in the infrastructure changes.
